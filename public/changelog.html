What's new.

Every update, fix and feature — documented as we build Scrabl in public.

Redesign
Oct 2026 · v0.4.0

New look, sharper focus

Rebuilt the whole front of the site around one idea: never run out of things to post. New minimal design in Outfit, repositioned messaging around consistency and your content shelf, and a cleaner sign-up and onboarding flow.

Launch
Aug 1, 2026 · v0.3.0

Public pages & domain live

About, Privacy, Terms, public roadmap, blog and Help Center all published. SSL activated — the site now loads over HTTPS, with a redirect for every visitor.

Feature
Jul 22, 2026 · v0.2.0

Early-access system & email verification

Full sign-up flow with verification codes and branded emails. MongoDB Atlas for storage, Resend for delivery, admin notifications on every new signup, and backend auto-deploy from GitHub.

Fix
Jul 20, 2026 · v0.1.1

Network & connectivity fixes

Forced IPv4 DNS to fix MongoDB and email on Nigerian networks, configured CORS, switched the Mongo connection string to standard format, and fixed an email-service syntax bug.

Launch
Jul 18, 2026 · v0.1.0

Initial launch

First landing page, dark theme with Lagos Gold, creator onboarding, a dashboard with profile and progress, early Studio UI, a mobile menu, and the Gemini engine wired into the backend.

Infrastructure
Jun 2026 · v0.0.1

Project started

Repository created, Node + Express backend initialised, first HTML/CSS prototypes, and the brand locked in — scrabl, the leaf, Lagos Gold and #scrabl26.
