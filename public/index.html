Support

Questions, answered.

How does Scrabl keep my shelf full?+

It turns your ideas, your niche, and what's already working into ready-to-post content — then keeps refilling the shelf so you never run out.

What if I run out of ideas?+

That's the whole point — you don't have to bring fresh ideas every day. Scrabl keeps the shelf stocked even on the days you're completely tapped out.

Will my posts still sound like me?+

Yes. Everything is written in your voice — your slang, your rhythm, your energy — through Voice DNA, which gets sharper the more you use it. Consistent, but never robotic.

Do I have to post every day?+

No. Scrabl makes daily posting effortless if that's your goal, but you move at your own rhythm. The full shelf is always there when you're ready.

Which platforms does it write for?+

X, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube — each one written native to how that platform actually works, not the same text pasted everywhere.

Is Scrabl built for African creators?+

Yes, specifically. Made in Akwa Ibom, for creators here first — it understands our slang, our culture and our rhythm instead of treating Africa as an afterthought.
