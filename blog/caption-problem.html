The consistency problem nobody talks about.

Making one good post is easy. Making one every day is what breaks people. Something is broken — and it's not you.

Elijah Saviour
Founder, Scrabl

Every post starts with a real moment. The hard part isn't the moment — it's doing it again tomorrow, and the day after. I built Scrabl because I was tired of running dry two weeks into every content streak.

Most tools "help" by making you post more. That just means more generic, robotic content that platforms bury and audiences scroll past. The grind gets easier to feed, but harder to survive.

Staying consistent actually takes three things: always having something to say, saying it in a way that sounds like you, and not spending an hour per post to get there. Miss any one and you quietly stop showing up.

Scrabl keeps a shelf stocked with all three — ideas ready to go, written in your voice, a tap away. Shorter prep, fewer rewrites, more posts that actually match the moment.

If you're spending more energy forcing yourself to post than making the thing, you're looking at the wrong problem.

Consistency shouldn't cost you your voice or your sanity. That's the whole reason Scrabl exists.

Never run dry again.

Keep a full shelf, in your voice, and just keep showing up.
