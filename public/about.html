About

We're building the tool that keeps
creators showing up.

Scrabl exists so you never run out of things to post — and never sound like a robot doing it.

The problem we couldn't let go of

Making one good post is easy. Making one every day — that's what breaks people. The blank screen, the "what do I even post," the quiet burnout. Most creators don't quit because they run out of talent. They quit because they run out of fuel.

And the tools that promised to help made it worse. "Post more, faster" just meant more generic, robotic content that platforms bury and audiences scroll past. The grind got easier to feed, but harder to survive.

What Scrabl does differently

Scrabl keeps a shelf of ready-to-post content stocked for you — pulled from your ideas, your niche, and what's already working. Open it, pick one, post. You stay consistent without the stress.

And the part that matters most: everything on that shelf sounds like you. Scrabl learns your voice — your slang, your rhythm, your energy — so consistent never means robotic. That's the line we refuse to cross.

We don't help you post more. We help you keep showing up, as yourself.

Built for us, here first

Scrabl is made in Akwa Ibom, for African creators first. It understands how we actually talk — the culture, the slang, the rhythm — instead of treating Africa as an afterthought the way global tools do. That's not a limitation. It's the whole point.

Still early, built in the open

Scrabl is in early access. We're building it with creators, not just for them — shaping it from real feedback, one honest step at a time. If that's the kind of thing you want to be early to, you're exactly who we're building for.

— Elijah Saviour, Founder
