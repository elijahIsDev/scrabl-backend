Getting started
What is Scrabl?

Scrabl keeps a shelf of ready-to-post content stocked for you — pulled from your ideas, your niche, and what's already working. Open it, pick one, post. The point isn't to post more; it's to help you stay consistent without facing a blank screen, and to make sure everything sounds like you, not a bot.

How do I get started?

Head to the sign-up page, create your account, and verify your email with the code we send you. Then you'll set up your voice and niche so Scrabl knows how you talk. That's it — your shelf starts filling from there.

I didn't receive my verification code

Check your spam, junk and promotions folders first. If it's still not there, go back to the sign-up page and request a new code. Still nothing? Email us at scrablmedia@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.

Is Scrabl free?

During early access, yes. We haven't set pricing yet. Early users will get the best deal and first access when we introduce paid plans — and you'll never be charged without your consent.

How it works
What does the shelf actually hold?

Posts ready to go — captions, hooks, short scripts and ideas built around your niche and your voice. Instead of starting from zero every day, you open Scrabl, pick what fits, tweak if you want, and post.

Will it really sound like me?

That's the part we care about most. Scrabl learns your voice — your slang, your rhythm, your energy — from the samples you give it during setup. Consistent should never mean robotic, and that's the line we refuse to cross.

What platforms does it support?

Content is tailored for Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, YouTube and Facebook. Each platform gets its own treatment — punchy for X, story-led for Instagram, professional for LinkedIn.

Does Scrabl post for me?

Not yet. For now you copy what you want and post it yourself. Auto-posting is on the roadmap for a future release.

Account & data
Who owns what Scrabl makes?

You do. Everything Scrabl generates for you is yours — we don't claim ownership over your ideas or outputs. Use them however you want.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data is stored securely with encryption at rest, we don't sell it, and we don't use your content to train AI. Read the full Privacy Policy for the details.

Can I delete my account?

Anytime. Email us at scrablmedia@gmail.com and we'll remove your data. No hard feelings.

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and we'll get back to you.
