5 hook formulas that actually stop the scroll.

Forget "did you know" and "here's why." These are the hook structures behind the most viral posts of 2026.

Elijah Saviour
Founder, Scrabl

Hooks are the first line of defense between your content and the scroll. If the top line doesn't land, nothing else matters. Here are five formulas that work when the feed is noisy and audiences are impatient.

1. The instant contrast. Start with what people expect, then switch to something they don't. Example: "Everyone says 'post more' — but that's exactly why your content disappears."

2. The personal confession. Say the thing creators are thinking but won't admit. Example: "I spent 2 hours on a caption and still didn't post it."

3. The small promise. Commit to one real outcome instead of vague success. Example: "How to write one caption that gets people to comment, in 60 seconds."

4. The misdirection. Start with a detail, then widen it. Example: "This screenshot isn't the reason I got the message — the caption was."

5. The audience mirror. Name who you're talking to, fast. Example: "Creators who hate generic AI captions: this is for you."

These hooks work because they make the reader feel seen before they decide whether to keep reading. The rest of the post should then deliver on that promise with a clear takeaway and a tiny next step.
